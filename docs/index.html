Folio

Read and listen to your books, on Android and Windows.

Folio is a reader for your own ebooks (EPUB and PDF) and audiobooks. Read a chapter, listen to the next, and it keeps your place between the two, word by word when the book has an exact sync.

Highlights, notes, bookmarks and your place in each book can stay the same on all your devices through your own Google Drive, if you turn that on.
